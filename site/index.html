About the numbers

  • Source: the daily per-prefecture bulletins of the Ministry of Development's fuel price observatory on fuelprices.gr, one PDF a day, read automatically. Daily from — to —. Prices are averages in euro per litre, VAT included.
  • Heating oil is the bulletin's “Diesel Θέρμανσης Κατ' οίκον”, delivered to the home, and is only reported from 15 October to 30 April, the season with reduced excise duty. “Greece (national average)” is the average the bulletin prints, weighted by each prefecture's sales.
  • The national figures match the EU Weekly Oil Bulletin's prices for Greece on almost every Monday since 2017, for unleaded 95, diesel and heating oil.
  • Super is hidden by default in the all-fuels chart: by late 2021 only a handful of stations in the whole country still sold it, so its averages say little.
  • Short gaps are days the site has no readable bulletin: days it never published, bulletins posted only as scanned images, and a technical outage from 26 to 29 January 2024.

From world markets to the pump

All three in euro per litre at the pump: the international wholesale price with Greek excise and VAT added, the Greek refinery price with VAT, and the pump price. The refinery moves a few days after the international price and the pump about a week after the refinery; the gaps between the lines are refining, distribution and retail margins. The international line ends about a week before the others because EIA publishes it with that delay.

How fast a price move reaches the pump

How the outlook works

  • The forecast combines three things known on the day: how far the pump price sits from the refinery price plus its usual margin, how much the refinery price moved in the last week, and how much the international price moved in the last week. It is refitted on all earlier data and checked against what actually happened, every day since January 2024.
  • International prices are New York Harbor wholesale diesel and gasoline from the US Energy Information Administration, which takes them from Refinitiv (LSEG) and publishes them about a week after the fact. The model only uses what had been published on each day, so its international input is always a week old; that is the honest version, and its backtest record above is measured that way. Refinery prices are the ELPE and Motor Oil prices from oil.gge.gov.gr, as collected by the open greek-oil-refinery-prices project.
  • Heating oil is only sold at its reduced rate from 15 October; in the first week of a season its forecast leans on the road diesel refinery price, which is the same fuel at a higher excise.
  • Autogas and Super are not covered: the refinery quotes autogas per tonne, and Super is no longer sold.
